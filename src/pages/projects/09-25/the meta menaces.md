> This project was the final project for the games design BTECH course for OCR. The client for this project was [iconika](https://iconika.io/)

This project was the first project I have done where I had a profesional client, iconika; or more exactly simon horton a representive memeber of the iconika team.

## project breif / overview
For this project our class was split into a group of 4, my team was essentially 2 3D modlers and 2 programmers. I was varied between tasks, here is what i did for the project:

> I had no part in the gameplay for the "harrison" partition of the gameplay, excluding game basics: power up selection; generation; enviromental models
- full ui design
    - main menu
        - and respective sub menus
        - pause menu
        - command menu
- the power up system
    - sprites (12/25 of them)
    - designed (10/25 of them)
    - programmed (25/25 of them)
    - balanced (25/25 of them)
    - ui design for the selection screen and the in game display
- player characters
    - all 3 of the base game characters
        - all of the sprites for these characters
        - all of the balancing and design for these characters (based off of the meta menaces main cast though)
- enemys
    - the 3 base enemys
        - all sprites for these enemys
        - all balancing and design, they are a take off the original not a one to one.
- codebase (these are using in all areas of the project)
    - libaries / plugins
        - I wrote a custom input system which i am still activly updating and maintaining past the last update of this page, [eevee](https://github.com/roxcelic/eevee)
        - I wrote a config libary used in tandom with unitys player prefs

## development history
> This will be catagorised into 3 development stages: pre dev; during dev; post dev.

### pre dev (planning)
This stage of the game all 4 members of my team worked on researching the ip and creating concept art. This phase was relativly short since we where given a detailed breif as well as already made character designs.

here is the breif i was given:
```
The constraints of this project that differ from the rest of the coursework we have done previously include:
- The idea
    - The idea behind the project will now be up to the client, which as described by the brief will be the story of the meta menaces
- The budget
    - We are being challenged to make a sort of “vertical slice” of a professional grade game with a budget of £0 and a time frame of 4 months
- The client
    - The client we are working for is a newly formed company meaning that they are less likely to have a streamlined process and collection of assets which could help us out. I am aware that we will be receiving a copy of book1 and a set of character sheets


The purpose of this project is to provide the company iconika with some references and ideas of how their world would perform within a game-like environment. This could also lead to possible internships with iconika in order to further their world and goals within the business space.

This does require us to stay faithful to the lore within the universe of the meta menaces, So anything we add that could be stated lore wise should be requested from the company themselves.
```
